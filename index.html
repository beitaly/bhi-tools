Discount Analyser
Interactive widget · Built per package
Flash-sale discount calculator. Takes the Prorata figures (AVG + MIN netto, pricing chain applied) and shows the new price, client saving in €, and remaining margin at any discount %. Breakeven is always 5.66% (1 − 1/1.06). Built fresh in each package chat — ask Claude to generate it from your Prorata sheet.

Excursion Booking Automation
Railway · Flask + Playwright
End-to-end excursion workflow: scrapes confirmed bookings from the VP partner portal, maps them to Servantrip or Escursì products, books automatically, cleans voucher PDFs, uploads to Google Drive, and delivers merged hotel + excursion PDFs to VP.
GET /sync-vp Scrape VP → populate Excursions sheet
GET /run Book all pending rows
GET /run/one?ref=Surname Book a single client
GET /merge?ref=VPREF Merge + email vouchers to VP
